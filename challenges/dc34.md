## We're baaaaack!
HHV CTF is back for DEF CON 34!

The fun begins later this week, so in preparation here's our list of recommended
tools/toys to bring if you plan on competing:

* 3.3V programmable dev board (like the rp2040/2350)
  * Our challenges are not 5V compatible, so no Arduino UNOs!
* Logic analyzer
* Serial-to-USB converter
* Jumper wires/mini-grabbers/aligator clips

As always, we'll have soldering irons and other tools, but everything is first
come first served.

See you all in Vegas!
