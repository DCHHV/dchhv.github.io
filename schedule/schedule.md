### Hands on Hardware Hacking Demos
WeAreAJ & PwnMonger
#### About
Ready to break some things? 🛠️ Drop by this Friday & Saturday for some hands-on hardware hacking demos by WeAreAJ and PwnMonger!

We’re covering:

* UART Exploration & Exploitation: Popping shells on commercial routers, security cameras, and a custom bedside patient monitor.
* Firmware Dumping: Ripping firmware off routers and DVRs (ch341a in action!).
* Hardware Persistence: Dropping an ESP8266 onto vulnerable pins.
* Timing Side-Channels: Basic timing side channel demo.
