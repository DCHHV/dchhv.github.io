## What is available

- Solder irons, solder, flux, leaded solder, helping hands
- Hot air stations
- Logic analyzers
- Voltmeters
- Microscopes, magnification lamps
- Disassembly tools, spludgers, screwdrivers, tweezers, wire strippers
- Items not publicly disclosed

## Source Pile

One of our favorite recurring wonders of DEFCON. This is a table. People drop off. People pickup. There is scavenging and there is dumping of items that need another life. Need a component? Can't bring something on the plane home? The Source Pile is your friend.
